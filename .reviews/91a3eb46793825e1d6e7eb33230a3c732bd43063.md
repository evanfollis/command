### Medium — Unbounded read on an authenticated hot path (`listLatestEvalRuns`) — remediated downstream
As committed, `evalTelemetry.ts::listLatestEvalRuns` reads **every** run JSON in each `runs/` dir (each ≤2 MB, but the run *count* is uncapped) to sort by `ts` and keep `[0]`, synchronously inside `getObservatorySnapshot` (every 15 s; `timed()`'s `Promise.race` cannot interrupt a sync fs loop) and via `GET /api/evals/summary` (no timeout/try-catch). As runs accumulate, an authenticated request fully reads N×(≤2 MB) and blocks the event loop — violates the "bounded reads" invariant. Mitigation: single-user authenticated surface. **Fixed downstream in `5b34b3f`** (`MAX_EVAL_REPORT_CANDIDATES_PER_PROMPT = 8`, `.slice(0,8)` before reading). Real defect at authoring; closed at HEAD.

### Medium — Healthy eval gate from three self-declared booleans (`collectEvalState`) — remediated downstream

Exactly the "baseline treated as acceptance evidence" hazard. **Fixed downstream** (`44ae9fe`, `5b34b3f`, `706894d`, `594d058`): HEAD `observatory.ts` `validateAcceptedBaselineEvidence` revalidates cases, provider provenance, and recomputes the aggregate. Real gap at authoring; closed at HEAD.
